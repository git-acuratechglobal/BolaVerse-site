Getting started

BolaVerse is a social football picks game where you compete with friends by making picks before kickoff, earning points, and climbing the leaderboard.

The app focuses on Southeast Asian football, featuring the ASEAN Championship, Indonesia Liga 1, Malaysia Super League, and the English Premier League.

No. BolaVerse is not a betting or gambling app. There is no money involved, no wagering, and no cash prizes. It's simply a football game where you compete with friends for fun.

Picks & competitions

Go to My Picks. At the top of the screen, swipe left or right to browse the available competitions — two competitions are displayed on each screen.

Tap the competition you want to follow. The page will automatically update, showing all upcoming matches for that competition. You can then make your picks for any available match.

Go to My Picks, select a competition, then open an upcoming match. Tap the score boxes to choose your predicted score.

Once you've selected your score, you don't need to do anything else — a short gray flash will appear a few seconds later to confirm your pick has been saved. You can edit your pick anytime before kickoff.

Yes. Go to My Picks, open the match, and simply choose a new score — your latest pick is automatically saved. You can change it as many times as you want before kickoff. Once the match starts, your pick is locked.

If you don't submit your pick before kickoff, you won't earn any points for that match.

Leagues

Go to My Picks and tap Create League. You can also go to My Leagues and tap the + button in the top-right corner. Choose a league name, complete the setup, then invite your friends.

Go to My Picks and tap Join League. Enter the 6-character league code, then tap Join to enter the league.

Go to My Picks, select the competition you want to play, then scroll down to the Featured Leagues section and tap Join League. You'll automatically compete against everyone who joined that public league for the selected competition.

When creating a private league, you'll be able to share your invitation using the league link, the league code, or your favorite social media apps.

If your league has already been created, open the league, go to Info, and share the invitation link or league code anytime.

For private leagues, friends' picks become visible once the match has started. Go to My Leagues, open your private league, then tap the match that is Live or Finished to see every member's pick for that match.

Yes. For a private league, open your league, go to Settings, then tap Leave League at the bottom of the page. For a public league, open your league, go to Info, then tap Leave League at the bottom of the page.

Scoring & leaderboard

You earn points by correctly picking the match outcome (home win, draw, or away win). If your pick matches the final result, you earn points. If you also guess the exact final score, your points for that match are multiplied by 2 (×2 bonus). Your total score is updated after the match has finished.

BolaVerse uses its own PIA (Prediction Impact Assessment) scoring system. Each match is worth a total of 100 points. Points are distributed according to the probability of each possible outcome before kickoff — expected results are worth fewer points, while less likely results are worth more points.

If you correctly predict the exact score, your points for that match are doubled.

The leaderboard is automatically updated after every match has finished and all points have been calculated.
