How it works

Three simple steps to become a legend.

Getting started takes less than a minute. Pick your score, earn points and watch every match become even more exciting.

STEP 01

Make your pick

Choose a competition, open any upcoming match, and tap the scoreline you're backing. Change your mind as many times as you like — right up until kickoff.

STEP 02

Earn points

Call the outcome right and you're on the board. Nail the exact final score and your points for that match are doubled.

STEP 03

Climb the table

The moment a match ends, the leaderboard updates. Watch yourself rise — or your friends pull ahead — league by league.

The scoring system

Every pick has a value.

Every match is worth 100 points. The harder a pick is to get right, the more points you can earn.

  • Favorites earn fewer points. Safer picks bring smaller rewards.
  • Underdogs earn more. Call an upset and score big.
  • Exact score = 2× points. Precision pays off.
